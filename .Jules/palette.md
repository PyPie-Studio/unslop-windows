## 2025-09-10 - Interactive Menu Feature Toggles Preview & Quick Reset
**Learning:** In terminal CLI/batch interfaces, stateful toggle sub-menus require explicit visual feedback showing the aggregated parameters/flags before execution, as users may lose track of configured states across toggles.
**Action:** Always provide a real-time "Active Flags" parameter preview bar and a 1-keypress Reset/Clear function (`[C]`) in terminal sub-menus.
